# SG-04A Mandatory Condition Disposition (approval draft)

All 12 conditions remain **OPEN** exactly as recorded in the SG-03D register. SG-04D adds finding-specific remediation notes to that authoritative register without changing designated evidence. This matrix translates them into proposed architecture boundaries and validation stages. Under SG-04R, each condition restricts activation of dependent capabilities; it does not block unrelated implementation. The authoritative [SG-03D register](../adr/SG-03D-m3-mandatory-conditions.md) records the prospective restrictions. Design statements are not validation evidence. The named owners are proposed role owners, not assigned individuals. A condition closes only when its listed controlled evidence exists, is reviewed, and is recorded in M3 evidence; a design statement or paper review alone is insufficient.
