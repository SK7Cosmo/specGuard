# SG-04A Mandatory Condition Disposition (approval draft)

All 12 conditions remain **OPEN** exactly as recorded in the SG-03D register. SG-04D adds finding-specific remediation notes to that authoritative register without changing the designated validation gates. This matrix translates them into the proposed architecture boundaries and review gates. The authoritative [SG-03D register](../adr/SG-03D-m3-mandatory-conditions.md) now records SG-04A document coverage/status alongside each gate; those descriptions are not validation evidence. The named owners are proposed role owners, not assigned individuals. A condition closes only when its listed controlled evidence exists, is reviewed, and is recorded in M3 evidence; a design statement or paper review alone is insufficient.
