# SG-03D Mandatory M3 Design Conditions

Every condition below is an M3 progression gate. Status remains **OPEN** until the listed validation evidence is recorded and reviewed. A dependent M4 component must not be implemented before its applicable gate is satisfied. Failure or inconclusive evidence keeps that component blocked. These conditions do not establish technical feasibility or product acceptance.
