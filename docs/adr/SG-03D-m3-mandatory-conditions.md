# SG-03D Mandatory M3 Design Conditions

At the time this register was created, every condition below was an M3 progression gate that blocked implementation of a dependent M4 component. SG-04R prospectively replaces that sequencing rule with the component activation policy below. The condition definitions, designated evidence, statuses and historical SG-04A/SG-04D records remain preserved. Every status remains **OPEN** until the designated evidence is recorded and reviewed. These conditions do not establish technical feasibility or product acceptance.

SG-04A and SG-04D record design-document coverage separately from validation status. Documentation does not prove the required controlled observations. SG-04D corrects design contracts and maps each reviewed finding to the affected requirements and gates; no controlled D01–D12 closure proof was run. All statuses remain **OPEN** pending the designated evidence and review. SG-04R does not reclassify prior evidence.

## SG-04R prospective progression and activation policy

This section supersedes the historical “Progression gate” column prospectively. The condition names, requirement mappings, designated evidence, status and SG-04A/SG-04D findings above remain as recorded. SG-04E and earlier evidence is not rewritten or reclassified. D01–D12 remain **OPEN** until their designated evidence has been produced and reviewed.

Implementation may begin when the relevant design contracts are approved. An OPEN condition does not block unrelated deterministic, manual, evidence-import or documentation work. Before a capability is activated, its applicable condition must have the designated reviewed evidence; a capability without that evidence stays disabled or fails closed. Implement applicable security controls from the first change. Develop critical controls using TDD with independently authored expected outcomes. An unavailable runner does not block other work. No untrusted repository code runs outside verified disposable isolation. No remote AI transmission occurs until redaction, current consent, exact payload and transport controls pass. No protected service is exposed until local authority controls pass. No condition passes without its designated reviewed evidence. P0 MVP completion remains prohibited until all 53 P0 criteria, all five MVP scenarios and all applicable mandatory gates have passed.

| Condition | Prospective activation restriction |
|---|---|
| D01 | Intake may be implemented behind a disabled boundary; accept or persist specifications only after the secret-class registry, pre-persistence inspection, sanitized rejection and exact accepted-byte controls pass. |
| D02 | Rule parsing and drafts may proceed with independent oracles; do not approve or use generated cases for verdicts until combined-bound, impossible-domain, conflict and explicit-outcome controls pass. |
| D03 | Runner approval workflow may be modeled and tested with inert fakes; do not dispatch repository commands until atomic single-use, replay, failure, restart and recovery controls pass. |
| D04 | Untrusted-content UI work may proceed with escaping controls from the start; do not display imported or repository content in an active view until hostile-content browser observations pass. |
| D05 | Evidence import and inspection may proceed; imported evidence cannot affect criterion verdicts until assertion relevance, provenance, freshness, integrity and reopen/export agreement pass. |
| D06 | Safe parser/Git implementation may proceed against pinned objects and strict bounds; enable each parser/intake path only after its malformed, oversized, no-fetch/no-escape and atomic-preservation evidence passes. Include SG-R032-AC02. |
| D07 | Non-executing analyzers may be implemented with syntax-only parsing; do not claim supported coverage for a stack until paired positive/negative fixtures, stable locations and unsupported-scope controls pass. |
| D08 | Session authority controls may be implemented and tested locally; do not start or expose a protected service until Host/Origin, bootstrap, proof expiry, revocation, restart and deny-before-disclosure controls pass. |
| D09 | Runner controller and limit policy may proceed without executing repository code; execution stays disabled until approved disposable Linux isolation, resource enforcement, network/credential/mount controls, teardown and recovery are verified. |
| D10 | Provider-independent deterministic/manual flows remain available; AI dispatch stays disabled until secret inspection/redaction, exact payload and endpoint consent, redirect blocking, bounded transport, response validation and sanitized failure pass. |
| D11 | Storage schema and deterministic repository work may proceed; persist audit/spec/evidence data only after transactional integrity, idempotency, rejected-source exclusion, history and recovery evidence pass. |
| D12 | Component work may proceed independently; do not claim integrated P0 readiness or completion until all 53 P0 criteria and five MVP scenarios have independent expected outcomes, joined reviewed evidence and every applicable gate passed. |
